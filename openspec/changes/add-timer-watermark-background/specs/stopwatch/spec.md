# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Show the team logo as a stopwatch watermark
The stopwatch interface SHALL display the Spånga handball logo as a subtle background watermark while keeping the timer readout and controls clearly legible and usable.

#### Scenario: View the stopwatch
- **WHEN** the stopwatch is displayed in its initial, running, paused, or startup-countdown state
- **THEN** the logo appears behind the stopwatch interface without obscuring its readout or controls
